About me

Hi, I'm Jose Renato.

And I love books.

I still remember reading under my desk during class, so immersed in whatever world I happened to be in that the lesson simply went on without me.

Somewhere along the way, I lost the joy of finding something great to read. School, university, medical school and residency took, with no ill intent, the time and headspace that reading needs. Social media made it worse. We started optimizing even literature: how many books you read, which ones, how long your to-read list is. Picking up a book turned into pressure, a high-stakes decision, and the plain pleasure of reading quietly disappeared.

It took me a while to fall in love with reading again. That's why I built Passages: to help people discover reading, or rediscover it, and to make literature feel like it belongs to everyone.

I'm a 28-year-old physician from Brazil, finishing my training in pediatrics in New York City. In my spare time I love to, well, read. I'm also dabbling in writing, and I hope one day to write good stories of my own. I share my home with my wife and two cats named after the galaxy's most famous siblings, Princess Leia and Luke.
